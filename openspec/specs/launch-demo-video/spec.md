# launch-demo-video Specification

## Purpose

Defines the launch video of the plugin: a short video, cut for a social feed, built around the plugin's one idea (don't delegate your reasoning, or you'll get a mug), with its format, its synthesized soundtrack and the checks it must pass before delivery.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The video is built around the idea
The video SHALL state, as on-screen type, the reminder "Don't delegate your reasoning." and "Or you'll get a mug.", preceded by the two facts it rests on: "You can't control what the model returns." and "You control what you delegate." It SHALL contain, in order: a hook in which the cat answers a request with a mug, the habit of asking again named as re-rolling, the idea, the plugin shown in a terminal (once a day, after the last response, gone at the next prompt), no more than two of the other mascots with the `/mascot` command, and an end card with the plugin's name and the install commands.

#### Scenario: Scene order
- **WHEN** the rendered video is scrubbed from start to end
- **THEN** the hook, the repetition, the idea, the terminal, the mascots and the end card appear in that order

#### Scenario: Hook
- **WHEN** the first three seconds are watched
- **THEN** the request and what came back ("You got a mug.") have both been shown

### Requirement: The video works with the sound off
Every line the video says SHALL appear as on-screen type large enough to read in a feed and SHALL stay on screen for at least two seconds; nothing SHALL be carried by audio alone.

#### Scenario: Muted
- **WHEN** the video is watched with no audio
- **THEN** the idea, what the plugin does and how to install it are all readable

### Requirement: The mascots are the real ones
Every picture of a mascot in the video SHALL be drawn by the plugin's own scene code, not redrawn for the video.

#### Scenario: Art changes
- **WHEN** a scene in `lib/scenes/` changes and the video is rendered again
- **THEN** the video shows the changed scene

### Requirement: Format and length
The video SHALL be rendered at 1920 by 1080, 30 frames per second, H.264 in an MP4 container with AAC audio, between 40 and 60 seconds long, to `docs/launch-v3.mp4`.

#### Scenario: Probe the file
- **WHEN** the output file is probed
- **THEN** it reports 1920x1080, 30 fps, h264 video, aac audio, and a duration between 40 and 60 seconds

### Requirement: Soundtrack is synthesized, not licensed
The soundtrack SHALL be generated by a script in this repository from code alone (no sampled or licensed audio), deterministically, from the same timeline file as the video, so that its accents land on the video's hits.

#### Scenario: Regenerate from source
- **WHEN** the soundtrack script is run twice
- **THEN** it writes the same bytes both times

#### Scenario: Accents
- **WHEN** the rendered video's audio is measured around each headline hit of the timeline
- **THEN** the track is louder right after the hit than right before it

### Requirement: The track ends with the picture
The loop SHALL stop when the end card starts, resolved to its home chord. The last sound of the track SHALL be the last hit of the timeline, which coincides with the last event on screen, and the track SHALL ring out to silence by the final frame.

#### Scenario: Ending
- **WHEN** the rendered video's audio is measured after the final hit
- **THEN** it is quieter in the last half second than right after the hit, and silent in the last 50 milliseconds

### Requirement: Video is verified before delivery
A script SHALL probe the rendered file, extract a frame after every scene start and every hit for human review, fail on a blank frame or a silent track, and the extracted frames SHALL be looked at before the video is delivered.

#### Scenario: Verification run
- **WHEN** the verification script is run on a fresh render
- **THEN** it writes the review frames and ends with OK
